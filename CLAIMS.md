## Status vocabulary

- `supported`: the stated scope is directly supported by fit-for-purpose evidence.
- `mixed`: relevant evidence or interpretations materially disagree.
- `tentative`: some support exists but important directness, quality, or coverage gaps remain.
- `unsupported`: reviewed evidence does not support the formulation.
- `not assessed`: appraisal is incomplete.
