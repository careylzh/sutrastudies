# Cross-reference: Taishō T. 1060 vs. the 84-line recitation text

The viewer's Chinese source of truth is the Taishō canon (CBETA TEI XML, T20n1060, phrases numbered 一–八十二 by the canon itself). Chinese musical renditions, including Imee Ooi's (黃慧音) *大悲咒* on the album *The Great Compassion Mantra* ([YouTube v7HioXeNCM8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v7HioXeNCM8)), sing the received 84-line recitation text (漢傳八十四句) instead. The video could not be opened from the build environment, so the comparison below is against the standard printed text of that edition, which is what the rendition follows; it does not verify the singer's pronunciation of any individual character.

**Result.** 54 of 82 Taishō phrases are identical in the recitation text. 28 differ, almost all by a homophonous or near-homophonous transliteration character (囉/羅, 唎/利, 耶/㖿, 怚/怛), which changes spelling but not the sound being transliterated. Two differences are substantive: phrase 16 gains five characters (薩哆那摩婆 is repeated), and phrase 81 is chanted as three lines with 曼哆囉鉢馱耶 respelt 漫多囉跋陀耶. The Chinese gloss (釋義) is unaffected, since both texts transliterate the same Sanskrit.
